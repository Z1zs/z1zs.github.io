PMark

Robust and distortion-free semantic-level watermarking through channel constraints.

PMark estimates semantic channels and constrains their joint sampling process to keep the watermark robust and distortion-free.

The problem

A semantic watermark should encode a reliable signal while leaving the text distribution—and therefore the writing quality—undisturbed.

PMark treats semantic watermarking as a constrained channel-sampling problem. It generates candidate tokens, estimates multiple semantic channel distributions, and partitions them into watermark-compatible regions. The channel constraints are enforced jointly during sampling and context updating, allowing PMark to maintain a detectable signal without the token-level distortion introduced by conventional biased decoding. The resulting design provides a clean route to robust, distortion-free semantic watermarking.
