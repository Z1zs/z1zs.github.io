CausalEmbed

Auto-regressive multi-vector generation in latent space for compact visual-document embeddings.

Jiahao Huo, Yu Huang, Yibo Yan, Ye Pan, Yi Cao, Mingdong Ou, Philip S. Yu, and Xuming Hu

CausalEmbed generates a sequence of compact latent vectors and compares documents and queries through late interaction.

The problem

Visual documents contain rich layout and text signals, but retrieval systems often pay for that richness with long token sequences.

CausalEmbed introduces autoregressive multi-vector generation directly in latent space. Given a visual document, a causal generator produces a compact sequence of embedding vectors that preserves complementary evidence for retrieval. The same representation supports late interaction with text queries, while test-time scaling lets the system trade additional latent computation for accuracy when needed. In the reported settings, CausalEmbed uses 30–155× fewer tokens than token-heavy visual document representations.

How it works

Encode the page

A visual document encoder turns layout, text, and image evidence into a shared latent representation.

Generate vectors causally

A lightweight autoregressive generator emits a sequence of complementary vectors, conditioned on what has already been generated.

Interact late

Multi-vector document embeddings preserve fine-grained matching with text queries without storing every visual token.
