MMNeuron

Discovering neuron-level, domain-specific mechanisms inside multimodal language models.

Jiahao Huo, Yibo Yan, Boren Hu, Yutao Yue, and Xuming Hu

Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

MMNeuron traces how multimodal information travels through domain-specific neurons in an MLLM.

The question

What makes a multimodal language model behave differently on documents, medicine, commerce, advertising, or remote sensing?

MMNeuron studies domain-specific neurons in multimodal large language models and asks how they support specialized visual–language behavior. The work identifies where these neurons appear, analyzes the mechanism through which they affect predictions, and proposes a three-stage view of domain-specific processing. A logit-lens analysis connects neuron activation to the model’s output space, while controlled manipulation shows that domain-specific neurons are not merely correlated with performance: changing them can shift domain accuracy by up to 10% in the reported experiments.

How it works

Find, explain, intervene.

Discover domain neurons

Locate neurons whose activation patterns distinguish one visual–language domain from the others.

Trace the mechanism

Use a three-stage interpretation and logit-lens analysis to connect internal activation to domain-specific outputs.

Test causality

Manipulate the discovered neurons and measure the change in domain performance to validate their functional role.
